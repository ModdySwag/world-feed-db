    We believe that new adventures begin with a live view of each location, where people can experience their next destination virtually before travelling there in person. This is what WorldLive App is designed for — to help people discover novel attractions all over the world and plan their trips.

    Start travelling around the globe at no cost. All content delivered through the WorldLive App is completely FREE. No airline tickets, no baggage fees, and no hotel reservations are required as you scope out your next destination.
